ABOUT

PRODUCT DESIGNER & TECHNOLOGIST

TVTanmoy VisionUI/UX • Product Designer
MAIN BIO // DOSSIERPROFILE / 01

I’m a UI/UX designer who enjoys turning complex ideas into simple, thoughtful digital experiences. I care about the details people often overlook, the decisions behind every interaction, and the little moments that make a product easier to use.

I approach design by understanding the problem before jumping into the pixels. I explore user journeys, organize information, and refine interfaces until the experience feels clear, calm, and natural to navigate.

I care about how people actually use a product: the confusing screen during a chaotic commute, the overlooked error state when offline, the extra step that could be cleanly eliminated, and the subtle tactile details that make an interface feel genuinely considered rather than rushed.

My work spans end-to-end product UX, interactive design systems, rigorous prototyping, and modern website builds. I also take genuine joy in bringing designs to life through clean front-end implementation, Framer, and custom code environments.

PHILOSOPHY & PRINCIPLES

HOW I THINK & MAKE

Three non-negotiable fundamentals that shape every product brief, user interface, and system architecture I build.

01 / DISCOVERY

Start with the why

Before opening any design tool or sketching components, I seek to understand what we’re solving, who we’re solving it for, and why it genuinely matters. Good design begins with better questions, not faster mockups.

Questions before pixels.

02 / HUMANITY

Design for real life

People interact with digital products while busy, distracted, multi-tasking, or in a hurry. I design specifically for edge cases, spotty connections, and cognitive friction—never just the static, unblemished showcase screen.

No perfect users here.

03 / SYNERGY

Better work together

Thoughtful software matures through candid cross-functional collaboration. I value rapid feedback, transparent rationale, and working lockstep with engineers and founders to turn abstract concepts into robust, shippable reality.

Better together.
